Privacy Policy — Spend Map

Effective date: 3 October 2026

Applies to: Spend Map for Android (com.spendmap), version 0.1.7 and later

The short version

Spend Map is an offline budgeting app. There is no account, no sign-up, and no server belonging to us. Everything you enter stays in a single file on your own phone. We cannot see your financial data, because it is never sent to us.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no servers, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no trackers. The app contains no third-party analytics or telemetry SDKs of any kind.

If the app crashes

If the app crashes, it saves a short technical report in the app's private storage on your phone: the error, the app version, the Android version and the phone model. It is meant to hold none of your budget data. It is never sent anywhere automatically and there is no reporting service behind it. A Share crash log row appears in Settings so that you can send it to us yourself if you want to, and you can read it in the share sheet first.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter — accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, goals, recurring payments and net-worth history — is written to a single JSON file in the app's private storage on your device:

/data/data/com.spendmap/files/spendmap-state.json

Android prevents other apps from reading that directory. The file is removed when you uninstall the app.

Purchases

Spend Map offers an optional paid subscription and a one-time lifetime purchase. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play Billing — we never see or receive your card number, billing address or any payment details. The app receives only a purchase/entitlement status ("active", "expired") from Google Play in order to unlock features, and caches that status locally on your device. Google's handling of the transaction is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Notifications

The app requests the POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission solely to show you local reminders about your own upcoming bills, generated on your device from the recurring payments you entered. No notification content leaves your phone, and nothing is sent through any server. You can decline the permission, or turn reminders off in Settings, and the rest of the app works normally.

Google Pay and Google Wallet payments (optional)

If you turn on Auto-capture Google Pay in Settings, the app first explains what it will do and then sends you to Android's Notification access screen, where you decide whether to allow it. With access granted, Android lets the app see the notifications on your phone. The app only looks at notifications from Google Pay and Google Wallet; from a payment notification it takes the shop name and the amount and saves them as a transaction in the app, on your phone. Every other notification is ignored, no notification text is kept, and nothing is sent anywhere. The feature is off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off, or withdraw Notification access in Android's settings, at any time.

Backups

Spend Map participates in Android Auto Backup, which may copy spendmap-state.json to your own Google account so your data survives moving to a new phone. Specifically:

  • Two things are included: that file, and a small record of when your free trial began and whether you have a plan (so reinstalling the app does not start a second free trial). The app's other local files, caches and preferences are excluded.
  • The trial start date is also kept in Google Play services Block Store on your phone, which keeps it through an uninstall while your phone's Google backup is on. It is a single date; nothing else is stored there.
  • Cloud backup is disabled on devices without a screen lock (disableIfNoEncryptionCapabilities). Where it does run, Android encrypts the backup end-to-end with your device lock credential, so Google cannot read it.
  • Device-to-device transfer (moving to a new phone during setup) copies the file directly between devices and never touches Google's servers.

This is your backup, in your Google account, not ours. You can turn Android backup off entirely in your system settings.

Internet access

Spend Map makes no network requests to us or to any third party for your data. The only components that touch the network are Google Play Billing (for purchase verification, above) and the Google Play Store itself.

The travel screen draws its globe on the device from map data bundled inside the app. It makes no network request.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share or disclose your data to anyone, because we do not have it.

Your control over your data

  • Export — Settings → Export data writes a JSON copy wherever you choose.
  • Import — Settings → Import data replaces the current state from a file.
  • Delete — uninstalling the app deletes the data file. To also remove the Google backup copy, delete the app's backup from your Google account settings.

Children

Spend Map is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated, and material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
